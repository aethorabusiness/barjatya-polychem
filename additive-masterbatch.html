Additive Masterbatch

Comprehensive functional additive solutions manufactured in Jaipur. Engineered to enhance polymer melt stability, eliminate surface friction, prevent UV photodegradation, and optimize processing economics.

Brochure Specification

All 10 Core Additives Formulated for High-Speed Conversion

Modern polymer conversion demands specialized additive packages to prevent line stoppages, improve product optical clarity, and protect finished plastics from harsh outdoor conditions.

Barjatya Polychem formulates the full suite of 10 industrial additive solutions listed in our official company profile. Produced on specialized compounding extruders, our additives ensure uniform active ingredient distribution at minimal let-down ratios.

Consult Formulation Specialist

1. Optical Brighteners (OB / OB-1)

Fluorescent whitening masterbatch neutralizing yellowish polymer undertones and imparting brilliant bluish-white reflectance in virgin and recycled plastics.

Films, Moulding & Recycled Polymers

2. Blowing & Foaming Agents

Exothermic and endothermic chemical blowing agents generating uniform closed-cell microcellular foam structures to reduce component weight and eliminate sink marks.

Weight Reduction: 10% – 30%

3. PP Clarifying & Nucleating

Advanced sorbitol-based clarifying masterbatches dramatically improving clarity, haze reduction, and flexural modulus in Polypropylene containers.

Haze Reduction & Cycle Time Shortening

4. Antistatic Masterbatch

Migratory and permanent antistatic agents dissipating surface electrostatic charges to prevent dust attraction on packaging and electronic trays.

Surface Resistivity: 10^9 – 10^11 ohms

5. UV Stabilizers (HALS)

Hindered Amine Light Stabilizers (HALS) and UV absorbers protecting polyolefins against UV photodegradation, chalking, and brittleness.

Greenhouse Films, FIBC & Crates

6. Antioxidants (Primary & Secondary)

Phenolic and phosphite synergistic blends preventing polymer chain degradation and MFI shifts during high-temperature extrusion cycles.

Thermal Processing Stability

7. Polymer Processing Aid (PPA)

Fluoropolymer based processing aids eliminating melt fracture (sharkskin), reducing die pressure, and lowering power consumption on blown film lines.

Eliminates Die Buildup & Melt Fracture

8. Slip Masterbatch (Erucamide / Oleamide)

High-purity fatty acid amides lowering the coefficient of friction (COF) on film surfaces to facilitate high-speed packaging bag conversion.

Dynamic COF < 0.20 Target

9. Antiblock Masterbatch (Synthetic Silica)

Micro-spherical synthetic silica and natural mineral antiblocking agents preventing film layers from sticking together on winding reels.

Synthetic & Natural Silica Grades

10. Flame Retardants (UL 94 Compliant)

Halogenated and non-halogen eco-friendly flame retardant concentrates delivering UL 94 V-0 and V-2 self-extinguishing ratings for electrical appliances.

UL 94 V-0 / V-2 Compliance
