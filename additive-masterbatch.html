Product Line 04

Additive Masterbatch

Specialty functional polymer modifiers engineered to enhance process efficiency, protect against thermal and UV degradation, and improve finished plastic product performance.

Product Breakdown

10 Functional Additive Solutions

Formulated to client specifications to resolve manufacturing challenges such as melt fracture, static buildup, yellowing, and weathering.

Additive 01

Optical Brighteners (OB)

Absorbs invisible ultraviolet radiation and re-emits blue fluorescence, masking polymer yellowness and imparting a brilliant optical white effect to films and moulding.

Additive 02

Blowing Agent

Endothermic and exothermic chemical foaming agents designed to generate uniform microcellular structures, reduce part weight, prevent sink marks, and improve thermal insulation.

Additive 03

PP Clarifying Agent

Nucleating and clarifying masterbatch for Polypropylene (PP) homopolymer and random copolymers, enhancing optical transparency, clarity, and structural flexural modulus.

Additive 04

Antistatic Agent

Dissipates electrostatic charge accumulation on plastic surfaces during manufacturing and storage, preventing dust adhesion on packaging, electronic housings, and films.

Additive 05

UV Stabilizers (HALS / Absorbers)

Hindered Amine Light Stabilizers (HALS) and UV absorbers protecting outdoor plastics from photo-oxidation, colour fading, embrittlement, and loss of tensile strength.

Additive 06

Antioxidant Masterbatch

Synergistic blends of primary phenolic and secondary phosphite antioxidants inhibiting thermo-oxidative degradation during high-temperature extrusion and extending polymer service life.

Additive 07

Polymer Processing Aid (PPA)

Fluoropolymer-based processing aids reducing die lip buildup, eliminating melt fracture (sharkskin), lowering extrusion backpressure, and improving line throughput.

Additive 08

Slip Masterbatch (Erucamide / Oleamide)

Migrates to the film surface to dramatically lower the Coefficient of Friction (COF), enabling smooth high-speed film winding, bag opening, and form-fill-seal (FFS) packaging.

Additive 09

Antiblock Masterbatch

Inorganic microscopic spacers creating micro-surface roughness to prevent adjacent plastic film layers from adhering (blocking) under winding tension or pressure.

Additive 10

Flame Retardants

Halogenated and non-halogenated flame retardant masterbatches suppressing combustion, reducing smoke generation, and preventing flaming drips in electrical and automotive applications.
