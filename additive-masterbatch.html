Product Line 04

Additive Masterbatch Solutions

Complete spectrum of 10 functional additive concentrates designed to modify polymer performance, enhance thermal stability, and optimize conversion efficiency.

Functional Modification

Engineered Additives for Demanding Conversion Lines

Barjatya Polychem formulates additive masterbatches that solve real-world processing challenges: bubble instability, melt fracture, electrostatic attraction, thermal degradation, and surface blocking.

Each additive concentrate is compounded on high-torque twin-screw extruders to ensure homogeneous dispersion and uniform let-down behavior in base polyolefins.
