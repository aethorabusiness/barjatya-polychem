Rutile TiO2 Concentrates

White Masterbatch

Premium Rutile Titanium Dioxide ($TiO_2$) masterbatches manufactured on dedicated white-only compounding lines. Delivering exceptional whiteness, high opacity, and thermal resistance for films, lamination, and moulding.

Brochure Specification

Maximum Opacity, Surface Gloss & Zero Agglomerates

Barjatya Polychem White Masterbatches are formulated with premium treated Rutile $TiO_2$ pigments loaded up to 75% in PE, PP, and PS virgin carrier resins.

Our strictly segregated white production lines eliminate the risk of dark pigment specks. By coating each $TiO_2$ crystal with specialized organic dispersants, we achieve high tinting strength, excellent dispersion without die buildup, and high thermal stability during extrusion.

Request Custom TDS

Multi-Layer & Barrier Film Grades

Formulated for liquid packaging, milk pouch films, courier bags, and high-speed co-extrusion blown films requiring total opacity and pinhole-free sealing.

TiO2 Loading: 60% – 75% | Ultra-Fine Dispersion

Extrusion Coating & Lamination

Specially developed to resist lacing and die buildup at high processing temperatures (up to 300°C) in paper coating and woven fabric lamination.

Lacing Resistant | High Thermal Stability

Injection & Blow Moulding Grades

High-gloss white masterbatches for cosmetic bottles, food containers, pharma caps, appliance housings, and household furniture with bright bluish undertones.

High Gloss | Bluish Tint Option

Food Contact & UV Weatherable Whites

Formulated with FDA compliant Rutile $TiO_2$ and outdoor UV light stabilizers to prevent yellowing and degradation in exterior furniture and agricultural sheeting.

FDA Food Contact | UV Weatherable
