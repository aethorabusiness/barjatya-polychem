Product Line 03

White Masterbatch Solutions

High-opacity Rutile Titanium Dioxide ($TiO_2$) concentrates delivering light barrier, surface gloss, and homogeneous dispersion across film and moulding applications.

Rutile TiO2 Technology

Superior Light Scattering & Dedicated Extrusion Lines

Barjatya Polychem formulates White Masterbatches with surface-treated Rutile Titanium Dioxide, ensuring high whiteness index, UV barrier protection, and high dispersion.

To prevent contamination from dark pigments, our facility operates dedicated separate compounding lines specifically for White Masterbatches, ensuring pristine brightness in flexible packaging, lamination films, and household goods.
