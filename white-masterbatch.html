Product Line 03

White Masterbatch

Premium Rutile Titanium Dioxide ($TiO_2$) concentrates delivering maximum opacity, whiteness index, good dispersion, and high surface finish for flexible films, lamination, and moulding.

Formulation Grades

High-Opacity Rutile $TiO_2$ Concentrates

Formulated on dedicated separate white compounding lines to ensure maximum optical whiteness and clean dispersion.

Films & Barrier

Blown & Barrier Film Grades

Engineered for single and multi-layer blown films, milk pouches, shopping bags, and agricultural films requiring high light barrier and opacity.

  • Clean film surface without pinholes
  • Excellent barrier and opacity performance
Lamination

Extrusion Coating & Lamination

Formulations for high-speed extrusion coating, paper lamination, and woven sack coating with minimal die buildup and excellent melt stability.

  • Stable at elevated extrusion temperatures
  • Low die build-up during long runs
Moulding

Injection & Blow Moulding

Concentrates formulated for household containers, bottles, furniture, cosmetic packaging, and appliances requiring high gloss and bright undertones.

  • High surface gloss and whiteness index
  • Available with blue or neutral undertone
