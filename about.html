About Barjatya Polychem

ISO 9001:2015 certified masterbatch manufacturer established in 2024, operating an 1,800 MT/year facility in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India.

Compounding Excellence

Formulating Quality Standard Compounds for Plastic Industries

Barjatya Polychem was founded in 2024 with a clear mandate: to deliver technically consistent masterbatch concentrates and mineral compounds that optimize plastic conversion processes.

Operating from the Jhotwara Industrial Area in Jaipur, our manufacturing plant incorporates high-dispersion Twin Screw Extruders, Single Screw Extruders, industrial Kneaders, and an in-house laboratory.

To prevent cross-batch contamination and guarantee shade purity, we maintain separate dedicated production machinery for White, Colour, Black, and Filler masterbatches.
