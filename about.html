Corporate Profile

About Barjatya Polychem

Established in 2024 in Jaipur, Rajasthan, Barjatya Polychem is an ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturer of high-quality polymer masterbatches and compounds with an annual capacity of 1,800 metric tons.

Foundational Purpose

Formulating Quality Standard Compounds for Plastic Industries

Barjatya Polychem was founded with a clear industrial objective: to produce compounds of the highest quality standard using advanced technology and experienced personnel.

Operating out of the Jhotwara Industrial Area in Jaipur, our manufacturing plant combines modern Twin Screw Extruders, Single Screw Extruders, and high-speed Kneaders with a developed in-house testing laboratory.

We operate separate dedicated machinery for White, Colour, Black, and Filler masterbatches. This operational segregation ensures pure pigment development and eliminates cross-batch contamination across all production runs.
