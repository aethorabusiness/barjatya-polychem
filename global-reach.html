Export Corridors & Ambition

Export Markets & Global Reach

Headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, Barjatya Polychem serves major domestic industrial hubs across India and actively exports to Nepal and Bangladesh, backed by export-grade packaging and international compliance.

Regional Manufacturing Hub

Strategic Location in Jaipur, Serving South Asian Industrial Converters

Located in Jhotwara Industrial Area, Jaipur, Barjatya Polychem benefits from direct connectivity to major freight corridors, dry ports (ICDs), and border trade routes.

Our current export operations focus on regular supplies of Colour, Black, White, Additive, and Filler masterbatches to polymer converters in Nepal and Bangladesh, supporting regional woven packaging, film extrusion, and blow-moulding industries.

With our 1,800 Tons/Year capacity and ISO 9001:2015 quality management system, we are strategically structured to fulfill expanding global demand with timely logistics, verified batch test certificates, and responsive customer service.
