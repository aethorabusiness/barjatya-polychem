Compounding Infrastructure

Manufacturing Plant & Machinery

Our Jaipur facility operates high-torque Twin Screw Extruders, Single Screw Extruders, high-speed Kneaders, and separate dedicated machinery for White, Colour, Black, and Filler masterbatches.

Plant Machinery

Core Production Equipment

High-shear dispersive compounding lines engineered for precise pigment wetting and melt homogenization.

Twin Screw Extruders

Co-rotating intermeshing twin-screw extruders providing controlled shear zones, modular kneading blocks, and thorough dispersion for organic and inorganic pigments.

High-shear masterbatch compounding

Single Screw Extruders

Continuous single-screw extrusion lines with temperature-regulated water cooling baths and high-speed strand pelletizers for uniform masterbatch granule cutting.

Uniform cylindrical micro-pelletizing

Dispersive Kneaders

Industrial dispersion kneaders for high-intensity preliminary compounding, coating pigments and functional additives into virgin carrier resins prior to extrusion.

Intensive pre-compounding mixing

Dedicated Machinery Segregation

Dedicated separate production machinery for White, Colour, Black, and Filler Masterbatches, preventing cross-contamination during product transitions.

Separate dedicated product lines
Production Flow

The Compounding Lifecycle

Conceptual overview of standard operations from raw material intake to final packaging.

01
Raw Materials

Selection of carrier resins, pigments, and functional additives.

02
Compounding

Dispersive pre-mixing in heavy-duty industrial kneaders.

03
Extrusion

Melt homogenization through twin-screw and single-screw lines.

04
Pelletizing

Water strand cooling and uniform micro-granule cutting.

05
Testing

Laboratory inspection for colour development and consistency.

06
Final Product

25kg industrial packaging for dispatch and export.
