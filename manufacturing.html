Compounding Technology & Capacity

Manufacturing Infrastructure

Our Jaipur compounding facility operates Twin Screw Extruders, Single Screw Extruders, and Kneaders with an annual capacity of 1,800 Tons, backed by strictly segregated production lines for color purity.

Plant Architecture

Engineered for Homogeneous Dispersion & Zero Cross-Contamination

The Barjatya Polychem manufacturing plant in Jhotwara Industrial Area, Jaipur, is designed around precision rheology, high-shear compounding, and strict process control.

Because different masterbatch formulations have distinct melt temperatures and pigment chemistry, we dedicate separate machinery specifically for:

Dedicated White MB Line
Dedicated Colour MB Line
Dedicated Black MB Line
Dedicated Filler Compound Line

This operational discipline ensures that sensitive white masterbatches and bright custom color batches never suffer from carbon black specking or mineral contamination.

Twin Screw Extrusion Line

Co-rotating intermeshing twin screws provide high-shear distribution, intensive devolatilization, and tight thermal profiling across multiple heating zones.

  • High pigment & additive loading capacity
  • Optimized shear stress for sensitive organic pigments
  • Uniform melt temperature control
Core for: Colour, Black & Additive MB

Single Screw Extruder Lines

Designed for continuous, high-volume mineral masterbatch and standard polymer compound processing with steady volumetric output and pressure control.

  • Precision temperature gradient along the barrel
  • Stainless steel water quenching bath
  • Rotary strand pelletizers with adjustable cut speed
Core for: Filler Compounds & White MB

Industrial Dispersive Kneaders

Heavy-duty dispersion kneaders ensuring deep wet-out and preliminary micro-dispersion of powdered pigments, additives, and virgin carriers.

  • Eliminates dry pigment agglomeration
  • Hydraulic ram pressure for uniform compaction
  • High-energy mechanical mixing
Core for: Pre-mixing & High Loading Formulations
Quality-Controlled Lifecycle

The 6-Step Manufacturing Process

From incoming raw material validation to final dispatch, our production follows strict ISO 9001:2015 process controls.

STEP 01
Raw Material Selection

Virgin polymer resins (LDPE, LLDPE, HDPE, PP), high-purity carbon blacks, rutile TiO2, and certified additives are inspected for MFI, moisture, and chemical purity.

STEP 02
Intensive Kneading

Raw pigments and additives undergo high-pressure dispersive kneading to break down pigment clusters and achieve thorough preliminary wetting with base polymers.

STEP 03
Twin-Screw Extrusion

The homogeneous mixture is fed into multi-zone extruders where controlled shear and temperature profiles melt and uniformly distribute particles through the polymer matrix.

STEP 04
Cooling & Pelletizing

Extruded polymer strands pass through temperature-regulated water baths and high-speed precision strand cutters, creating uniform, dust-free cylindrical granules.

STEP 05
Laboratory Inspection

Random lot samples are tested in our laboratory for Spectrophotometer delta-E, Melt Flow Index (MFI), dispersion quality, and thermal resistance before batch release.

STEP 06
Packaging & Dispatch

Finished masterbatches are packed into heavy-duty 25kg multi-layer moisture-barrier bags, palletized, and prepared for domestic transport or export to Nepal & Bangladesh.
