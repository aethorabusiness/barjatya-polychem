Colour Masterbatch

Tailored colour concentrates formulated on dedicated compounding lines in Jaipur. Featuring standard shades, precision RAL/Pantone matching, metallic, pearlescent, thermochromic, and UV-stabilized outdoor grades.

Brochure Specification

Engineered Shades with High Opacity & Consistent Let-Down Ratio

Barjatya Polychem colour masterbatches are formulated with high-quality organic and inorganic pigments, dispersed thoroughly within virgin carrier resins (PE, PP, PS, ABS, EVA) using co-rotating twin-screw extruders.

Whether formulating vibrant packaging films, durable rotomoulded water tanks, household injection items, or fine PP filaments, our compounds offer superior lightfastness, migration resistance, and thermal stability.

Standard & Custom Shades

Over 1,000+ formulated shades across reds, blues, greens, yellows, oranges, violets, and browns, with fast delta-E turnaround for bespoke customer color targets.

Matching: RAL, Pantone & Physical Plaques

Specialty Visual Effects

Premium aesthetic finishes including metallic aluminum flakes, pearlescent shimmer, glow-in-the-dark phosphorescent, and temperature-sensitive thermochromic masterbatches.

Outdoor Weatherable & UV Grades

Formulated with high lightfastness pigments (Blue Wool Scale 7-8) and UV HALS stabilizers to prevent fading in outdoor furniture, crates, and agricultural films.
