Colour Masterbatch Solutions

Tailored colour concentrates formulated across standard catalog shades, bespoke laboratory matching, outdoor weatherable grades, and special aesthetic effects.

Formulation Scope

Bespoke Matching & High-Dispersion Formulations

Barjatya Polychem formulates colour masterbatches engineered for polyolefin carriers (LDPE, LLDPE, HDPE, PP) and engineering thermoplastics according to client application demands.

Whether formulating high-opacity solids for industrial packaging, translucent tints for household wares, or weather-resistant shades for outdoor furniture, our laboratory matches target shades using digital spectrophotometers and pilot extrusion trials.
