Calcium Carbonate Mineral Compounds

Filler Compounds

High-purity surface-coated Calcium Carbonate ($CaCO_3$) masterbatches compounded on dedicated lines in Jaipur. Formulated to provide anti-fibrillation in PP woven sacks, reduce injection cycle times, and optimize manufacturing unit costs.

Brochure Specification

Surface-Treated Micro-Calcite for Smooth Melt Flow

Barjatya Polychem Filler Compounds incorporate ultra-fine, surface-coated calcium carbonate ($CaCO_3$) with stearic acid modifiers, pre-kneaded and extruded with virgin polyolefin carriers (PP / LLDPE / HDPE).

Our dedicated filler compounding line ensures uniform particle distribution, eliminating tape snapping on high-speed circular looms and preventing screw barrel abrasion while providing mechanical reinforcement.

PP Woven Sacks & Raffia Grade

Anti-fibrillation filler compound acting as a processing modifier to prevent tape splitting on high-speed tape lines while maintaining high tensile strength.

CaCO3 Loading: 70% – 80% | Anti-Splitting

PE Blown Film & Liner Grade

Ultra-fine micronized calcite compound designed for shopping bags, garbage bags, and agricultural films, improving opacity, stiffness, and printability.

CaCO3 Loading: 70% – 78% | High Opacity

Injection Moulding Modifiers

High-thermal-conductivity mineral masterbatch that shortens moulding cycle times, minimizes part shrinkage, and increases dimensional rigidity in household goods.

Shortens Mould Cooling Cycle Time

Thermoforming & Sheet Extrusion

Mineral compound improving sheet sag resistance, heat deflection temperature (HDT), and barrier rigidity in disposable thermoformed food trays and containers.

Rigidity & Heat Deflection Enhancement
