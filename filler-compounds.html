Product Line 05

Calcium Carbonate Filler Compounds

Surface-treated micronized CaCO₃ mineral masterbatches formulated for PP woven sacks, raffia anti-fibrillation, and flexible film cost optimization.

Mineral Compounding

High Loading & Smooth Extrusion Flow

Barjatya Polychem manufactures CaCO₃ Filler Masterbatches formulated with coated ultrafine calcium carbonate and high-purity virgin polyolefin carrier resins.

Widely utilized across PP woven sacks, HDPE shopping bags, and injection moulded parts, our filler compounds increase stiffness, eliminate tape splitting (anti-fibrillation), and reduce formulation raw material costs without sacrificing line speed.
