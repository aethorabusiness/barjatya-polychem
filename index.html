Manufacturing Infrastructure

The Compounding Lifecycle & Dedicated Machinery

Operating Twin Screw Extruders, Single Screw Extruders, and Kneaders with separate dedicated machinery for White, Colour, Black, and Filler masterbatches.

Jaipur Manufacturing Plant

Twin Screw Extrusion & Kneader Infrastructure

01 / STAGE

Raw Material Selection

Rigorous inspection of virgin carrier polymers (PE, PP, PS) and certified organic/inorganic pigments to ensure chemical compatibility and purity.

02 / STAGE

Dispersive Kneading

High-torque industrial kneaders provide intensive preliminary shearing to wet pigments and break agglomerates prior to extrusion.

03 / STAGE

Twin Screw Extrusion

Co-rotating twin-screw compounding lines melt and homogenize formulations under precise temperature and shear profiles.

04 / STAGE

Strand Pelletizing

Water bath strand cooling and high-precision rotary cutters create uniform, dust-free cylindrical masterbatch granules.

05 / STAGE

Laboratory Inspection

Quality verification using spectrophotometers, Melt Flow Index (MFI) testers, and dispersion checks under ISO 9001:2015 protocols.

06 / STAGE

Packaging & Dispatch

Standard 25kg moisture-barrier laminated industrial packaging with batch test reports for domestic dispatch and export corridors.
