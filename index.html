About Barjatya Polychem

Formulating Quality Standard Compounds for Plastic Industries

Established in 2024 in Jaipur, Rajasthan, Barjatya Polychem (BP) manufactures masterbatches and compounds to meet the evolving demands of plastic processing industries.

Equipped with Twin Screw Extruders, Single Screw Extruders, high-speed Kneaders, and a developed testing laboratory, we formulate polymer additives and color concentrates tailored to customer requirements.

Our manufacturing setup utilizes separate dedicated machinery for White, Colour, Black, and Filler masterbatches to prevent cross-contamination and ensure shade purity.

The Compounding Lifecycle

01
Raw Materials

Selection of carrier resins, pigments, and functional additives.

02
Compounding

Dispersive pre-mixing in heavy-duty industrial kneaders.

03
Extrusion

Melt homogenization through twin-screw and single-screw lines.

04
Pelletizing

Water strand cooling and uniform micro-granule cutting.

05
Testing

Laboratory inspection for colour development and consistency.

06
Final Product

25kg industrial packaging for dispatch and export.
