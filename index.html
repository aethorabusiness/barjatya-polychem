About Barjatya Polychem

Formulating Quality Standard Compounds for Plastic Industries

Established in 2024 in Jaipur, Rajasthan, Barjatya Polychem (BP) is dedicated to manufacturing masterbatches and compounds of the highest standard.

Equipped with state-of-the-art Twin Screw Extruders, Single Screw Extruders, high-speed Kneaders, and a fully developed testing laboratory, we formulate polymer additives and color concentrates tailored precisely to our clients' processing specifications.

Our manufacturing setup utilizes separate dedicated machinery for White, Colour, Black, and Filler masterbatches to eliminate batch-to-batch cross-contamination and ensure exact pigment dispersion.

The 6-Step Compounding Lifecycle

STEP 01
Raw Material Selection

Polymer carriers, pigments, and additives tested for purity and MFI.

STEP 02
Intensive Kneading

High-shear dispersive pre-mixing in heavy-duty kneaders.

STEP 03
Twin-Screw Extrusion

Co-rotating twin-screw compounding with precise thermal profiling.

STEP 04
Cooling & Pelletizing

Strand bath cooling and uniform micro-pellet cutting.

STEP 05
Laboratory Testing

Spectrophotometer delta-E, MFI, dispersion, and thermal checks.

STEP 06
Packaging & Dispatch

Moisture-proof 25kg multi-layer industrial packaging.

Quality Assurance & Testing

Developed Laboratory & Modern Testing Equipment

Quality at Barjatya Polychem is governed by our ISO 9001:2015 certified management framework. Every masterbatch lot is verified for optical and mechanical consistency.

Our in-house developed laboratory performs comprehensive testing on incoming polymer resins, pigments, and finished compounds to ensure full alignment with customer processing temperatures and machine requirements.

Colour Matching & Spectrophotometry

Digital CIELAB colour analysis for tight delta-E tolerance and bespoke shade reproduction.

Melt Flow Index (MFI)

Precision rheological evaluation to match resin flow in extrusion and moulding machines.

Pigment Dispersion Testing

Filter pressure value (FPV) and blown film plaque checks for agglomerate-free compounds.

Thermal Stability & Ash Content

High-temperature polymer endurance testing and mineral filler verification.
