High Jetness Carbon Black Concentrates

Black Masterbatch

Manufactured on dedicated compounding lines using fine-particle furnace carbon blacks. Delivering superior UV protection, high jetness, thermal stability, and smooth surface finish across pipes, films, and injection moulding.

Brochure Specification

Formulated for High Tinting Strength & Extrusion Performance

Barjatya Polychem Black Masterbatches are engineered with carbon black loadings ranging from 30% to 50%, pre-dispersed through high-shear twin-screw extruders.

Our dedicated black production line eliminates cross-contamination risks while guaranteeing uniform carbon dispersion without unmolten specks or pinholes, meeting IS 4984 pipe standards and critical thin-gauge film requirements.

Request Custom TDS

Pressure Pipe & Conduit Grades

Formulated with P-type fine particle furnace blacks for HDPE/MDPE water supply pipes, agricultural drip lateral tubes, and telecommunication ducts (IS 4984 compliant).

Carbon Loading: 35% – 45% (IS 4984 / ISO 4427)

Blown Film & Garbage Bag Grades

High-dispersion black concentrates designed for micro-thin shopping bags, agricultural mulch films, silage wraps, and heavy-duty industrial sacks without screen clogging.

Carbon Loading: 40% – 50% | High Dilution

High Jetness Moulding Grades

Deep black, high gloss masterbatches for automotive under-bonnet parts, exterior trim, consumer electronics, battery cases, and home appliances.

Piano Black Jetness | PP / ABS / PE Base

FDA Food Contact & Conductive Grades

Engineered with high purity, low-PAH carbon blacks compliant with food contact packaging standards, as well as specialized electro-conductive grades for ESD protection.

Food Contact Compliance & Antistatic
