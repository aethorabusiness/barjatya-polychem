Masterbatch Manufacturing in Jaipur: Regional Hub for Plastics Industry

How Jaipur's strategic industrial infrastructure supports 1,800 MT/year compounding capacity and regional export supply corridors.

Direct Answer: Situated in Jhotwara Industrial Area, Jaipur, Rajasthan, Barjatya Polychem operates a modern 1,800 MT/year masterbatch manufacturing facility equipped with Twin Screw, Single Screw, Kneaders, and an in-house quality testing laboratory, supplying polymer converters throughout North India and active export markets in Nepal and Bangladesh.

1. Strategic Geographic Position

Jaipur serves as a premier industrial and freight hub in western and northern India, offering direct highway and rail connectivity to raw material petrochemical complexes in Gujarat and consumer plastic conversion hubs in Rajasthan, Delhi-NCR, Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, and Punjab.

2. Export Corridors to Nepal & Bangladesh

Barjatya Polychem's facility is structured for export packaging (25kg moisture-barrier laminated bags with palletization) and multi-modal container transport, enabling steady supply lines to woven sack and film manufacturers in Nepal and Bangladesh.
