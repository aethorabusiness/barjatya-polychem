White Masterbatch: Rutile Titanium Dioxide ($TiO_2$) for Barrier Opacity

Understanding refractive index, light scattering physics, and dedicated extrusion lines for high-opacity white concentrates.

Direct Answer: Rutile Titanium Dioxide ($TiO_2$) is the premier white pigment for plastics due to its high refractive index (2.73), which efficiently scatters visible light to provide maximum whiteness, opacity, and UV barrier performance in thin polymer films and moulded components.

1. Rutile vs. Anatase Crystal Form

While Titanium Dioxide occurs in both Anatase and Rutile crystalline modifications, Rutile $TiO_2$ is preferred in industrial masterbatch compounding because of its higher refractive index, superior photo-stability, and greater resistance to outdoor weathering.

2. Critical Importance in Packaging Films

In flexible packaging applications such as milk pouches, edible oil films, and lamination substrates, high opacity is necessary to shield light-sensitive nutrients from photodegradation. White masterbatch formulated with pure Rutile $TiO_2$ ensures uniform opacity even at low let-down ratios.

3. Dedicated Compounding Lines

Because white masterbatch is sensitive to trace contamination, Barjatya Polychem operates dedicated separate compounding lines specifically for White Masterbatches, ensuring pristine brightness and zero cross-colour specks.
