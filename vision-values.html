Operating Principles

The Five Pillars of Our Manufacturing Ethos

How our vision translates into daily operational discipline across our Jaipur manufacturing plant.

1. Quality Standard & Technology

Operating Twin Screw Extruders, high-shear kneaders, and separate dedicated compounding lines under an ISO 9001:2015 quality framework to ensure pigment dispersion and zero contamination.

2. Meeting Market Requirements

Continuously reformulating masterbatch recipes to match evolving resin grades, recycled plastic blends, higher line speeds, and regional regulatory safety standards.

3. Total Customer Satisfaction

Partnering directly with converters to deliver bespoke color matching, on-site technical support, rapid sample prototyping, and stable melt-flow characteristics.

4. Honouring Commitments

Upholding transparent B2B relationships with dependable delivery schedules, consistent batch-to-batch let-down ratios, and transparent commercial pricing.

5. Clean Plant & Safety Culture

Maintaining disciplined plant housekeeping, dust containment systems, and rigorous raw material storage to ensure a clean, safe, and productive manufacturing environment.
