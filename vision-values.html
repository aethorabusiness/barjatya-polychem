Vision & Core Operating Values

"To be a dependable partner in masterbatches and polymer compounds by delivering consistent quality, technical responsiveness, and responsible manufacturing."

Operating Principles

Our Five Core Pillars

Quality Standard Formulations

Strict adherence to ISO 9001:2015 testing protocols across every batch produced in our Jaipur plant.

Customer Application Centricity

Customizing carrier resins and additives to match each client's specific conversion machinery and requirements.
