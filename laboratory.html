ISO 9001:2015 Quality Protocols

Developed Testing Laboratory

Our in-house quality assurance laboratory in Jaipur is equipped with modern analytical instruments to ensure precise delta-E color matching, rheological consistency, and zero agglomeration.

Testing Infrastructure

Strict In-House Batch Testing & Analytical Control

At Barjatya Polychem, quality assurance is an active operational discipline applied to every production batch rather than an afterthought.

Our laboratory technicians use calibrated testing equipment to qualify raw pigments, carrier polymers, and finished masterbatches. This prevents downstream processing issues like die buildup, film punctures, or color drift on our clients' production lines.

ISO 9001:2015 Quality Traceability

Every outgoing consignment is tagged with a batch number and accompanied by a verified Certificate of Analysis (COA) documenting MFI, ash content, and delta-E values.

Analytical Equipment

Modern Testing Instruments in Our Jaipur Facility

High-accuracy measurement instruments ensuring complete compliance with international ASTM, ISO, and DIN testing benchmarks.

CIELAB Spectrophotometer

Digital benchtop reflectance and transmittance spectrophotometer for computer color matching (CCM) and tight tolerance shade reproducibility.

ΔE ≤ 0.5 strict color tolerance

Melt Flow Indexer (MFI)

Precision automated plastometer measuring melt flow rate (MFR) according to ASTM D1238 / ISO 1133 to ensure matching viscosity in customer moulds.

Standard: ASTM D1238 / ISO 1133

Filter Pressure Value (FPV) Tester

Evaluates the dispersion quality and presence of undispersed pigment agglomerates to avoid screen clogging during thin film blowing and fiber spinning.

Standard: DIN EN 13900-5

High-Temp Muffle Furnace

Determines exact mineral filler concentration ($CaCO_3$, talc, TiO2 loading) and polymer ash residue under ASTM D5630 standards.

Standard: ASTM D5630 / ISO 3451

Two-Roll Test Mill & Pilot Press

Simulates commercial processing shear and compression to prepare test plaques for visual color matching, opacity, and weatherability inspection.

Plaque & Sheet Prototyping

Halogen Moisture Analyzer

High-sensitivity moisture detection ensuring that masterbatch and filler pellets have moisture levels below 0.1% to prevent surface foaming during processing.

Moisture Target: < 0.10%
