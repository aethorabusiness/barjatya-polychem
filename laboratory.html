Developed Laboratory & Testing

Our ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management system guides all stages of raw material qualification, colour development, physical property evaluation, and final lot verification.

Testing Competence

Modern Testing Equipment for Consistent Polymer Quality

The laboratory at Barjatya Polychem is equipped to evaluate both raw materials and finished masterbatch compounds.

Our technicians conduct digital spectrophotometer colour readings, Melt Flow Index (MFI) measurements, dispersion evaluations, and moisture testing to verify that each production batch matches customer-specified technical requirements.
