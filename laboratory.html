Laboratory & Quality Management

Operated under an ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management system, our developed testing laboratory in Jaipur performs rigorous raw material evaluation, digital colour matching, and finished compound testing.

Testing Infrastructure

Developed Laboratory & Modern Testing Equipment

Quality control at Barjatya Polychem operates under an ISO 9001:2015 certified management system.

Our in-house developed laboratory performs testing on polymer carriers, colour matching, and finished masterbatches to ensure compatibility with client processing requirements.

We verify pigment dispersion, Melt Flow Index (MFI) characteristics, and thermal stability to ensure that each batch meets customer specifications.

Quality Testing Protocols

In-House Testing & Verification Capabilities

Key testing procedures performed by our quality team in Jaipur.

Colour Matching & Spectrophotometry

Digital spectrophotometers and computerized software to evaluate reflectance curves, undertones, and shade accuracy against target swatches.

Digital colour development

Melt Flow Index (MFI) Testing

Rheological verification to ensure the masterbatch carrier melts and distributes smoothly within the target host polymer matrix.

Flow rate & viscosity control

Pigment Dispersion Analysis

Sample film blowing and press plaque inspection to verify the absence of undispersed agglomerates, gels, or specks.
